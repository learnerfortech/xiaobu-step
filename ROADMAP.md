# Xiaobu Step roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not a delivery promise or fixed schedule. Priorities may change when real usage evidence is available.

## Now — make the local version dependable

- Keep the existing local-first family workflow usable.
- Verify task creation and editing, photo submission, parent review, state read-back, and recovery after restart.
- Make installation limits and the trusted-LAN security boundary clear.
- Resolve bundled asset provenance and licensing questions.
- Add repeatable automated checks without presenting syntax checks as full product tests.

## Next candidates — choose one based on observed need

- Improve the daily task view and reduce the number of steps needed by parents and children.
- Explore homework-image import as an editable draft. The original image and uncertain fields must remain visible; nothing should become a child task before parent review.
- Explore a simple, manually confirmed mistake/redo record that does not depend on AI.

These candidates are not approved as a simultaneous feature bundle.

## Later — separate evaluations required

- AI-assisted homework recognition or checking, after accuracy, privacy, external data handling, and running costs are measured.
- Android and iOS packaging, after device capabilities and maintenance requirements are tested.
- Cloud sync, multiple families, accounts, and commercial plans, only after authentication, tenant isolation, data migration, backup, support, and operating costs are designed and validated.

## Not a current goal

- Automatic grading as the source of truth.
- Leaderboards, forced streaks, or punitive reward mechanics.
- A broad course marketplace or a feature-heavy family super-app.

The product should reduce coordination work and help children participate more independently. Feature count is not a success metric.
